# TOEFL iBT Templates, Model Answers & Rubrics

Reference for the `/toefl` skill (Writing & Speaking free-text). Completed in a later phase.
Contains: answer-structure templates, model answers, and the **scoring rubrics** Claude uses to
grade the user's free-text responses.

> TOEFL scale: total 0–120 (Reading/Listening/Speaking/Writing = 0–30 each). Speaking & Writing
> are scored on a 0–4/0–5 rubric, then converted. Real audio is out of scope — Speaking is
> graded from the user's text outline/script.

## Writing — Integrated (rubric 0–5)
- **Task:** read a passage + hear (transcript) a lecture → write a summary of how the lecture
