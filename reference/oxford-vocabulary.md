# Oxford Vocabulary Pool (Oxford 3000/5000, CEFR-tagged)

A graded vocabulary pool based on the **Oxford 3000** (≈A1–B2) and **Oxford 5000** (≈B2–C1),
the primary source for `/quiz` vocabulary questions and `/toefl` vocab items. Aligned with
the user's Anki deck (Oxford 3000, tier A1 in progress). **Filled in gradually** per unit — do
not assume tier A1 is complete.

> Columns: **Word · POS · CEFR · Meaning · Arti · Example** (Meaning = definisi English sederhana, Arti = terjemahan Indonesia). The CEFR tag = the official Oxford tier.
> When writing a unit, add that unit's themed word batch to the matching level table.
> **Cek level di PDF resmi** `reference/sources/oxford-3000-by-cefr.pdf` / `oxford-5000-by-cefr.pdf`
> (lihat `reference/official-sources.md`). Kata yang tidak ada di Oxford 3000/5000 → tabel
> **Di luar Oxford** di bawah (CEFR = `—`).

## A1
| Word | POS | CEFR | Meaning | Arti | Example |
|------|-----|------|---------|------|---------|
| wake up | phr v | A1 | stop sleeping | bangun tidur | *I **wake up** at five o'clock.* |
| meal | n | A1 | food eaten at one time | waktu makan / hidangan | *Breakfast is my favourite **meal**.* |
| routine | n | A1 | the normal order of things you do | rutinitas | *My morning **routine** starts at six.* |
| relax | v | A1 | rest and stop working | bersantai, istirahat | *I **relax** by reading at night.* |
| prepare | v | A1 | make something ready | menyiapkan | *I **prepare** breakfast every morning.* |
| trip | n | A1 | a visit to a place and back again | perjalanan, jalan-jalan | *We took a short **trip** to Bandung.* |
| arrive | v | A1 | reach a place | tiba, sampai | *We **arrived** at the hotel at nine.* |
| stay | v | A1 | live in a place for a short time | menginap, tinggal sementara | *We **stayed** with my aunt for two nights.* |
| miss | v | A1 | be too late for a bus, train, etc. | ketinggalan | *I **missed** the train by five minutes.* |
| remember | v | A1 | have something in your mind from the past | ingat | *I **remember** my first trip abroad.* |
| forget | v | A1 | not remember | lupa | *She **forgot** her phone at home.* |
