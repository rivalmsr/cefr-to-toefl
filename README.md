# learn-english — English Knowledge Base (→ TOEFL iBT)

Personal knowledge base to (1) become fluent in English and (2) reach an official **TOEFL
iBT** score for graduate (S2) admission at the **Institute of Science Tokyo**. Materials are
**written by Claude**, staged by CEFR level, read in **Obsidian**, and drilled with adaptive
Claude Code skills. Explanations are in **English (immersion)**.

> **Secondary project.** Primary focus is Japanese (`../learn-japan/minna-no-nihongo-i`).
> Kept light and incremental. See `CLAUDE.md` for rules and `.claude/skills/*/SKILL.md` for
> skill details.

## Folder map

```
lessons/unit-XX.md   themed integrated units (grammar+vocab+reading+phrases) — source of truth
reference/           vocab pools (Oxford/AWL), collocations, grammar ref, synonyms, TOEFL templates
progress/            diagnostic result + weakness trackers + session history
.claude/skills/      /diagnostic, /practice, /toefl
```

## How to use

1. **Run `/diagnostic` first** → sets your CEFR level (writes `progress/diagnostic.md`).
2. Read the units for your level in `lessons/` (in Obsidian).
3. **`/practice`** — daily adaptive drills from the units (12 questions, exam mode).
4. **`/toefl`** — text-based TOEFL iBT mock (Listening via transcript; audio out of scope).

## Curriculum roadmap (CEFR A1 → C1)

Each **unit** is one themed note bundling grammar + vocabulary (Oxford/AWL tier) + a short
reading + speaking/writing phrases + a "TOEFL link". Unit count per level is a target, not
fixed. The active starting level is set by `/diagnostic`.

### Level A1 — Foundations (Oxford 3000, tier A1)
| Unit | Theme | Grammar focus | Function |
|------|-------|---------------|----------|
| A1-01 | People & introductions | be (am/is/are), subject pronouns, a/an | introduce yourself |
| A1-02 | Family & possessions | possessive 's, this/that/these/those, plural nouns | describe belongings |
| A1-03 | Daily routines | present simple, adverbs of frequency | talk about routines |
| A1-04 | Places in town | there is/are, prepositions of place | describe a place |
| A1-05 | Free time | can/can't (ability), like + -ing | talk about hobbies |
| A1-06 | Food & shopping | countable/uncountable, some/any, how much/many | order & shop |

### Level A2 — Elementary (Oxford 3000, tier A2)
| Unit | Theme | Grammar focus | Function |
|------|-------|---------------|----------|
| A2-01 | Past experiences | past simple (regular/irregular), time markers | tell a past story |
| A2-02 | Travel & holidays | be going to, present continuous for future | make plans |
| A2-03 | Comparisons | comparatives & superlatives | compare things |
| A2-04 | Health & advice | should/shouldn't, have to | give advice |
| A2-05 | Describing experiences | present perfect (ever/never/just/yet) | share experiences |
| A2-06 | Quantity & food | too/enough, a lot of/a little | discuss amounts |

### Level B1 — Intermediate (Oxford 3000, tier B1 + early AWL)
| Unit | Theme | Grammar focus | Function |
|------|-------|---------------|----------|
| B1-01 | Education & goals | present perfect vs past simple | talk about study goals |
