# Unit 2 — Past Experiences: Past Simple (CEFR A2)

**Focus:** Bisa menceritakan **kejadian yang sudah selesai di masa lalu**, misalnya liburan,
akhir pekan, atau pengalaman pertama. Unit ini menambah **past simple** (Core Inventory A1–A2):
*was / were* untuk keadaan, dan kata kerja bentuk lampau (V2) untuk kegiatan, termasuk
negatif *didn't* dan tanya *Did …?*. Bentuk V2 yang kamu hafalkan di `/quiz-v` sekarang
dipakai di dalam kalimat. Tema vocabulary: **perjalanan dan pengalaman**.

> **Quick summary:** (1) **Past simple of _be_** — **was** (I/he/she/it) dan **were**
> (you/we/they) untuk **keadaan di masa lalu**; negatif (*wasn't / weren't*), tanya
> (*Was / Were …?*); *was/were* **tidak** dipakai bersama kata kerja lain (*~~I was go~~*).
> (2) **Past simple of other verbs** — kegiatan yang **sudah selesai** pada waktu tertentu di
> masa lalu; kata kerja beraturan **-ed** (aturan ejaan *lived, studied, stopped, played*),
> kata kerja tidak beraturan **V2** (*went, ate, saw, bought, left …*), bentuknya **sama untuk
> semua subjek**; negatif **didn't** + kata kerja dasar; tanya **Did** + subjek + kata kerja
> dasar; **penanda waktu** *yesterday, last week/month/year, … ago, in 2020, when I was …* —
> dibandingkan dengan *for / since* sampai sekarang (Unit 1). (3) **Vocabulary:** perjalanan
> & pengalaman (trip, arrive, stay, miss, remember, memory, experience, abroad, finally…).
> (4) **Reading:** cerita pendek akhir pekan di Bandung. (5) **Frasa Speaking/Writing** untuk
> bercerita tentang masa lalu. (6) **TOEFL link.**

## Grammar

### Point 1 — Past simple of _be_: was / were

**Aturan**
- **Kegunaan:** menyebut **keadaan** di masa lalu: perasaan, tempat, cuaca, atau sifat
  (*I **was** tired*, *The hotel **was** small*, *We **were** in Bandung*). Pasangannya di
  present simple adalah *am / is / are*.
- **was** → *I / he / she / it* (dan satu orang/benda): *I **was** at home. My mother **was**
  happy.*
- **were** → *you / we / they* (dan subjek jamak): *They **were** late. My friends **were**
  there.*
- **Negatif** — *kegunaan:* bilang keadaan yang **tidak** benar di masa lalu. **wasn't /
  weren't**: *The food **wasn't** expensive. We **weren't** tired.*
- **Pertanyaan** — *kegunaan:* menanyakan keadaan di masa lalu. **Was / Were** + subjek …?
  Jawaban singkat: *Was the beach crowded?* → *Yes, it **was**. / No, it **wasn't**.*
- **Hanya untuk keadaan.** Kalau kalimatnya tentang **kegiatan** (go, eat, buy, visit), jangan
  pakai *was/were*. Pakai kata kerja V2 (Point 2).

**Contoh**
- ✅ *I **was** very tired after the trip.*
- ✅ *The streets **were** crowded on Saturday.*
- ✅ *It **wasn't** cold, but it **was** windy.*
- ✅ ***Were** your parents at home yesterday?* — *No, they **weren't**.*

**Catatan**
- ⚠️ Kesalahan yang sering muncul untuk penutur bahasa Indonesia adalah *~~I was go to
  Bandung~~*. Ini mirip kebiasaanmu menambah *has* di Unit 1 (*~~has always late~~*). Kalimat
  dengan kata kerja kegiatan **tidak butuh** *was*: ✅ *I **went** to Bandung.*
- *Cara cek:* kalau sesudah *was/were* ada **kata sifat / tempat / benda** (*tired, at home, a
  student*), berarti sudah benar. Kalau sesudahnya **kata kerja** (*go, eat*), buang *was*-nya
  dan ubah kata kerjanya ke V2.

| Kalimatnya tentang… | Pola | Contoh |
|---|---|---|
| **keadaan** (tired, happy, crowded, at home) | **was / were** + adjective/tempat | *The beach **was** crowded.* |
| **kegiatan** (go, eat, buy, visit) | **V2** saja | *We **went** to the beach.* |
| kegiatan, **negatif** | **didn't** + kata kerja dasar | *We **didn't go** to the beach.* |
| kegiatan, **pertanyaan** | **Did** + subjek + kata kerja dasar | ***Did** you **go** to the beach?* |

### Point 2 — Past simple of other verbs: -ed, V2, didn't, Did …?

**Aturan**
- **Kegunaan:** menceritakan **kegiatan yang sudah selesai** pada **waktu tertentu di masa
  lalu**: *kemarin, minggu lalu, dua tahun lalu, waktu aku kecil*. Inilah tense utama untuk
  **bercerita** (liburan, pengalaman, sejarah).
- **Bentuknya sama untuk semua subjek.** Tidak ada *-s* untuk *he/she/it*: *I **visited**, she
  **visited**, they **visited***. Ini lebih mudah daripada present simple.
- **Kata kerja beraturan → tambah -ed.** Aturan ejaan:

  | Kata kerja berakhiran… | Aturan | Contoh |
  |---|---|---|
  | biasa | + **-ed** | *work → worked, visit → visited, stay → stayed* |
  | **-e** | + **-d** saja | *live → lived, arrive → arrived* |
  | konsonan + **-y** | **y → i** + -ed | *study → studied, try → tried* |
  | vokal + **-y** | + -ed (y tetap) | *play → played, enjoy → enjoyed* |
  | 1 vokal + 1 konsonan (kata pendek) | konsonan **digandakan** | *stop → stopped, plan → planned* |

- **Kata kerja tidak beraturan → V2 (dihafal).** Daftar lengkap dan polanya ada di
  `reference/irregular-verbs.md` (latihan di `/quiz-v`). Yang paling sering dipakai untuk
  bercerita:

  | V1 | V2 | V1 | V2 | V1 | V2 |
  |---|---|---|---|---|---|
  | go | **went** | eat | **ate** | see | **saw** |
  | have | **had** | take | **took** | come | **came** |
  | buy | **bought** | leave | **left** | meet | **met** |
  | get | **got** | make | **made** | find | **found** |
  | sit | **sat** | lose | **lost** | spend | **spent** |

- **Negatif** — *kegunaan:* bilang sesuatu **tidak terjadi** di masa lalu. **didn't** +
  **kata kerja dasar** (V1). Tanda lampaunya sudah ada di *did*, jadi kata kerjanya kembali ke
  bentuk dasar: *I **didn't buy** anything.* (bukan *~~didn't bought~~*)
- **Pertanyaan** — *kegunaan:* menanyakan apa yang terjadi di masa lalu. **Did** + subjek +
  **kata kerja dasar** …? Jawaban singkat: *Did you **enjoy** the trip?* → *Yes, I **did**. /
  No, I **didn't**.* Dengan kata tanya: ***Where did** you **stay**? **What did** she **buy**?*
- **Penanda waktu** — *kegunaan:* memberi tahu **kapan** kejadiannya (dan menjadi petunjuk
  bahwa tense-nya past simple):
  - *yesterday*, *yesterday morning*
  - **last** + waktu: *last night, last week, last month, last year* (tanpa *the*, tanpa *on*)
  - waktu + **ago**: *two days ago, three years ago* (*ago* ditaruh **sesudah** lamanya waktu)
  - **in** + tahun/bulan: *in 2019, in July*; **on** + hari: *on Saturday*
  - *when I was a child*, *when we arrived*

**Contoh**
- ✅ *We **visited** my aunt in Bandung **last** weekend.*
- ✅ *She **went** to Japan **two years ago**.* (bukan *~~ago two years~~*)
- ✅ *I **didn't take** many photos.* (bukan *~~didn't took~~*)
- ✅ ***Did** your brother **come** with you?* — *No, he **didn't**.*
- ✅ ***What did** you **eat** there?* — *We **ate** batagor.*

**Catatan**
- ⚠️ Kesalahan dari quiz kamu (`/quiz-t`): *~~My mother doesn't bought new clothes~~*. Setelah
  kata bantu (*do / does / did*), kata kerjanya **selalu bentuk dasar**. Aturannya sama dengan
  *~~Does your father often cooks~~* → *cook* di Unit 1. Untuk lampau: ✅ *She **didn't buy***.
- ⚠️ Kesalahan dari `/quiz-v` kamu: V1 ditulis di kolom V2 (*~~leave~~* → **left**, *~~sit~~*
  → **sat**, *~~find~~* → **found**), dan V2/V3 tertukar (*~~eaten, ate~~* → **ate, eaten**).
  Di kalimat past simple yang **positif**, kata kerjanya harus **V2**: *I **left** at six.*
  Ingat urutannya: **V2 = past simple** (tanpa kata bantu), **V3 = sesudah have/has**
  (Unit 1).
- **Past simple atau present perfect (Unit 1)?** Lihat **penanda waktunya**:

  | Penanda waktu | Artinya | Tense | Contoh |
  |---|---|---|---|
  | *yesterday, last year, 3 years ago, in 2019* | sudah **selesai**, waktunya jelas | **past simple** | *I **lived** in Bogor **in 2019**.* (sekarang sudah tidak) |
  | *for three years, since 2019* (sampai sekarang) | **masih** berlangsung | **present perfect** | *I **have lived** in Depok **since 2019**.* (sekarang masih) |

  Jangan pakai *ago* dengan *have/has*: *~~I have visited Bali two years ago~~* → ✅ *I
  **visited** Bali two years ago.*

## Vocabulary — Trips & experiences

**Tujuan:** kata-kata untuk **menceritakan perjalanan dan pengalaman**. Pasangkan dengan
grammar Point 1 (keadaan: *was tired*) dan Point 2 (kegiatan: *arrived, stayed*).

Kumpulan kata bertema (Oxford tier A1–A2, dicek di PDF Oxford 3000 by CEFR; CEFR `—` = tidak
ada di Oxford 3000/5000). Daftar lengkapnya juga ada di `reference/oxford-vocabulary.md`.

| Word | POS | CEFR | Meaning | Arti | Example |
|------|-----|------|---------|------|---------|
| trip | n | A1 | a visit to a place and back again | perjalanan, jalan-jalan | *We took a short **trip** to Bandung.* |
| arrive | v | A1 | reach a place | tiba, sampai | *We **arrived** at the hotel at nine.* |
| stay | v | A1 | live in a place for a short time | menginap, tinggal sementara | *We **stayed** with my aunt for two nights.* |
| miss | v | A1 | be too late for a bus, train, etc. | ketinggalan | *I **missed** the train by five minutes.* |
| remember | v | A1 | have something in your mind from the past | ingat | *I **remember** my first trip abroad.* |
| forget | v | A1 | not remember | lupa | *She **forgot** her phone at home.* |
| memory | n | A2 | something you remember from the past | kenangan | *That holiday is my favourite **memory**.* |
| experience | n | A2 | something that happened to you | pengalaman | *Climbing the mountain was a great **experience**.* |
| abroad | adv | A2 | in or to another country | ke/di luar negeri | *I went **abroad** for the first time last year.* |
| suddenly | adv | A2 | quickly and not expected | tiba-tiba | ***Suddenly**, it started to rain.* |
| finally | adv | A2 | after a long time | akhirnya | *We **finally** arrived at midnight.* |
| souvenir | n | — | a thing you buy to remember a place | oleh-oleh, cendera mata | *I bought a **souvenir** for my sister.* |

## Reading

**Tujuan:** melihat *was/were*, V2, *didn't* dan *Did …?* dipakai bersama **dalam satu cerita**.
Teks berbahasa Inggris di TOEFL yang berbentuk cerita atau sejarah hampir selalu memakai past
simple.

> **A weekend in Bandung**
>
> Last weekend, Rina and her brother **went** to Bandung by train. They **left** Jakarta early
> in the morning, but the train **was** late, so they **arrived** at noon. They **stayed** with
> their aunt. On Saturday, they **visited** a tea garden. The air **was** cool and the view
> **was** beautiful. Rina **took** a lot of photos, but her brother **didn't take** any because
> he **forgot** his phone at the house. In the evening, they **ate** batagor and **bought**
> souvenirs for their parents. On Sunday, they **were** tired, but they **were** happy. "**Did**
> you **enjoy** the trip?" their mother **asked** when they **came** home. "Yes, we **did**,"
> Rina **said**. "It **was** a great experience."

*(Sambil membaca, perhatikan: **was/were** hanya untuk keadaan (late, cool, tired); kegiatan
memakai **V2** (went, left, took, ate); sesudah **didn't / Did** kata kerjanya kembali ke
bentuk dasar (take, enjoy).)*

## Speaking / Writing phrases

**Tujuan:** kerangka kalimat siap pakai untuk **bercerita** — tinggal isi bagian *…*.

- **Membuka cerita:** *Last weekend, I …* · *Two years ago, I …* · *When I was a child, I …*
- **Urutan kejadian:** *First, we …* · *Then / After that, we …* · *Finally, we …*
- **Keadaan & perasaan:** *It was …* · *I was really …* · *The … wasn't very …*
- **Hal tak terduga:** *Suddenly, …* · *Unfortunately, I missed / forgot / lost …*
- **Menutup cerita:** *It was a great experience.* · *I still remember …*
- **Bertanya:** *What did you do last weekend?* · *Where did you go?* · *Did you enjoy it?*

> Selalu tulis **subjek** di setiap klausa (*after that **we** went …*, bukan *~~after that
> went~~*). Ini kebiasaan yang sama dengan Unit 1.

## TOEFL link

**Tujuan:** menunjukkan di bagian tes mana past simple benar-benar terpakai.

_Format TOEFL iBT 2026 (lihat `reference/toefl-2026-spec.md`)._

- **Take an Interview (Speaking):** pertanyaan seperti *"Tell me about a time when …"* atau
  *"Describe a trip you remember"* harus dijawab dengan past simple. Pola **Membuka → Urutan →
  Menutup** di atas cukup untuk jawaban ±45 detik.
- **Write for an Academic Discussion (Writing):** kamu perlu **contoh dari pengalaman
  pribadi** untuk mendukung pendapat (*When I was in high school, I joined …, and I learned
  …*). Contoh itu ditulis dalam past simple.
- **Read an Academic Passage & Listen to an Academic Talk:** teks sejarah atau penemuan ilmiah
  (*In 1928, a scientist discovered …*) memakai past simple. Penanda waktu (*in 1928, ago,
  later*) membantu menjawab soal urutan kejadian.
- **Build a Sentence (Writing):** sering menguji urutan kata pertanyaan lampau: ***Where did
  you stay**…?* (bukan *~~Where you did stay~~*), dan *didn't* + kata kerja dasar.
