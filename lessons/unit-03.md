# Unit 3 — Future Plans: be going to & Present Continuous (CEFR A2)

**Focus:** Bisa membicarakan **rencana ke depan**, misalnya akhir pekan, liburan, atau acara
keluarga. Unit ini menambah dua cara bicara tentang masa depan (Core Inventory A1–A2):
**be going to** untuk niat/rencana, dan **present continuous** (*am/is/are + -ing*) untuk
rencana yang **sudah diatur** (sudah janjian, sudah pesan tiket). Keduanya butuh **am/is/are**,
jadi unit ini juga melatih kebiasaanmu yang sering **lupa *be*** (*~~we sick~~*, *~~I going
to~~*). Tema vocabulary: **liburan, perjalanan, dan acara**.

> **Quick summary:** (1) **be going to** — **am/is/are + going to + kata kerja dasar** untuk
> **niat/rencana** yang sudah diputuskan (*I'm going to visit my grandmother*) dan **prediksi
> dari bukti yang terlihat** (*Look at those clouds. It's going to rain*); *be* **wajib**
> (*~~I going to~~*), sesudah *to* = kata kerja dasar (*~~going to visited~~*); negatif
> (*isn't / aren't going to*), tanya (*Are you going to …?*, *What are you going to do?*).
> (2) **Present continuous for future** — **am/is/are + V-ing** + **waktu** untuk rencana yang
> **sudah diatur** (janji, tiket, jadwal: *I'm meeting Andi at seven tomorrow*); aturan ejaan
> **-ing** (*make → making, stop → stopping, lie → lying*); negatif & tanya; **penanda waktu
> masa depan** *tomorrow, tonight, this weekend, next week/month, in two days, soon*;
> perbedaan *going to* (niat) vs present continuous (sudah diatur). (3) **Vocabulary:** liburan,
> perjalanan & acara (plan, holiday, ticket, flight, book, pack, invite, celebrate, wedding,
> guest…). (4) **Reading:** pesan rencana liburan Lebaran. (5) **Frasa Speaking/Writing**
> untuk membicarakan rencana. (6) **TOEFL link.**

## Grammar

### Point 1 — be going to: plans and predictions

**Aturan**
- **Kegunaan:** menyebut **niat atau rencana** yang **sudah kamu putuskan** sebelum bicara
  (*Aku mau / akan …* — keputusannya sudah ada di kepala). Contoh: *I'**m going to study**
  Japanese next year.*
- **Bentuk:** **am / is / are** + **going to** + **kata kerja dasar** (V1).

  | Subjek | be | going to | V1 |
  |---|---|---|---|
  | I | **am** ('m) | going to | visit |
  | he / she / it | **is** ('s) | going to | visit |
  | you / we / they | **are** ('re) | going to | visit |

- **Negatif** — *kegunaan:* bilang kamu **tidak berniat** melakukan sesuatu. **am not / isn't /
  aren't going to** + V1: *We **aren't going to** stay at a hotel.*
- **Pertanyaan** — *kegunaan:* menanyakan rencana orang lain. **Am / Is / Are** + subjek +
  **going to** + V1 …? Jawaban singkat: *Are you going to travel this year?* → *Yes, I **am**.
  / No, I'**m not**.* Dengan kata tanya: ***What are** you **going to do** this weekend?*
- **Prediksi dari bukti** — *kegunaan:* menebak apa yang **akan terjadi** karena **buktinya
  sudah terlihat sekarang**: *Look at those dark clouds. It'**s going to rain**.* · *The bus is
  very slow. We'**re going to be** late.*

**Contoh**
- ✅ *I'**m going to visit** my grandmother next month.*
- ✅ *My brother **is going to buy** a new laptop.*
- ✅ *They **aren't going to come** to the party.*
- ✅ ***Are** you **going to watch** the match tonight?* — *Yes, I **am**.*
- ✅ *Hurry up! We'**re going to miss** the train.* (prediksi: sudah terlambat sekarang)

**Catatan**
- ⚠️ **Jangan lupa *be*.** Bahasa Indonesia cukup bilang *aku mau pergi*, jadi mudah tertulis
  *~~I going to visit~~*. Ini kebiasaan yang sama dengan *~~we sick~~* → *we **were** sick*
  (Unit 2) dan *~~my brother always tired~~* → *is always tired* (Unit 1). Di tulisan
  diagnostic kamu juga ada *after that going to …* (tanpa subjek dan tanpa *be*).
  ✅ *After that, **I'm going to** …*
- ⚠️ **Sesudah *to* = kata kerja dasar.** Tanda masa depannya sudah ada di *going to*, jadi
  kata kerjanya **tidak** diberi -s, -ed, atau V2: *~~She is going to visits~~*, *~~We are going
  to went~~* → ✅ *She is going to **visit***, *We are going to **go***. Aturannya sama dengan
  *doesn't / didn't + kata kerja dasar* (Unit 1 & 2).
- *Cara cek* — hitung **tiga bagian**: (1) **am/is/are** cocok dengan subjek, (2) **going to**,
  (3) **kata kerja dasar**. Kalau satu bagian hilang atau berubah, kalimatnya salah.

  | ❌ | Bagian yang salah | ✅ |
  |---|---|---|
  | *~~I going to cook~~* | (1) *be* hilang | *I'**m** going to cook* |
  | *~~She are going to cook~~* | (1) *be* tidak cocok | *She **is** going to cook* |
  | *~~She is going to cooks~~* | (3) bukan V1 | *She is going to **cook*** |
  | *~~Are you going cook?~~* | (2) *to* hilang | *Are you going **to** cook?* |

- Kata **going** di sini **tidak** berarti "pergi". Karena itu *going to go* itu benar:
  *I'm going to **go** to Bali.* (Sering juga cukup ditulis *I'm going to Bali*, lihat Point 2.)

### Point 2 — Present continuous for future arrangements

**Aturan**
- **Kegunaan:** menyebut rencana yang **sudah diatur dengan orang lain atau sudah dipesan**:
  sudah janjian, sudah beli tiket, sudah ada jadwal. Biasanya ada **waktu atau tempat** yang
  jelas. Contoh: *I'**m meeting** Andi at the station **at seven tomorrow**.*
- **Bentuk:** **am / is / are** + **V-ing** + **penanda waktu masa depan**. Bentuknya sama
  dengan present continuous untuk "sedang", jadi **penanda waktunya** yang menunjukkan bahwa
  ini masa depan: *I'm cooking **now*** (sedang) vs *I'm cooking **tonight*** (rencana).
- **Aturan ejaan -ing:**

  | Kata kerja berakhiran… | Aturan | Contoh |
  |---|---|---|
  | biasa | + **-ing** | *visit → visiting, go → going, fly → flying* |
  | **-e** (tidak dibaca) | buang **e** + -ing | *make → making, leave → leaving, come → coming* |
  | **-ie** | **ie → y** + -ing | *lie → lying, die → dying* |
  | 1 vokal + 1 konsonan (kata pendek) | konsonan **digandakan** | *stop → stopping, plan → planning, get → getting* |

- **Negatif** — *kegunaan:* bilang sesuatu **tidak** dijadwalkan. **am not / isn't / aren't**
  + V-ing: *She **isn't working** next Monday.*
- **Pertanyaan** — *kegunaan:* menanyakan jadwal atau janji orang lain. **Am / Is / Are** +
  subjek + V-ing …? ***What are** you **doing** this weekend?* · ***When is** your brother
  **leaving**?* — *He's **leaving** on Friday.*
- **Penanda waktu masa depan** — *kegunaan:* memberi tahu **kapan** rencananya:
  - *tomorrow*, *tomorrow morning*, *tonight*
  - **this** + waktu: *this weekend, this Friday, this evening*
  - **next** + waktu: *next week, next month, next year* (tanpa *the*, tanpa *on*)
  - **in** + lamanya waktu: *in two days, in a week* (= dua hari **lagi**)
  - *soon*, *later*, *on Saturday*, *at 7 p.m.*

**Contoh**
- ✅ *We'**re flying** to Medan **next Friday**.* (tiketnya sudah dibeli)
- ✅ *My parents **are coming** to visit **this weekend**.*
- ✅ *I'**m not working tomorrow**, so we can meet.*
- ✅ ***Are** you **coming** to the wedding **on Sunday**?* — *Yes, I **am**.*
- ✅ *The guests **are arriving in two hours**.*

**Catatan**
- ⚠️ **Lagi-lagi *be*.** *~~I meeting Andi tomorrow~~* → ✅ *I'**m** meeting Andi tomorrow.*
  Sesudah subjek harus ada **am/is/are**, baru V-ing.
- ⚠️ **Jangan campur *be* dengan kata kerja dasar.** *~~I am meet Andi tomorrow~~* → ✅ *I am
  **meeting***. Polanya mirip dengan *~~was get up~~* di Unit 2: kalau ada *am/is/are/was*, kata
  kerja sesudahnya harus **-ing** (atau tidak ada kata kerja sama sekali, mis. *I am **happy***).
- ⚠️ **Jangan pakai present simple untuk rencana pribadi.** Bahasa Indonesia *Besok aku ketemu
  Andi* tidak punya penanda tense, tapi *~~Tomorrow I meet Andi~~* salah → ✅ *Tomorrow **I'm
  meeting** Andi.* (Present simple untuk masa depan hanya untuk jadwal resmi, mis. *The train
  **leaves** at 6* — tidak diuji di unit ini.)
- **going to atau present continuous?** Sering keduanya boleh. Bedanya:

  | Situasinya… | Pakai | Contoh |
  |---|---|---|
  | **niat / keputusan**, belum ada janji atau tiket | **be going to** | *I'**m going to visit** Japan one day.* |
  | **sudah diatur**: janji, tiket, jadwal, waktu jelas | **present continuous** | *I'**m flying** to Tokyo on 3 May.* |
  | **prediksi** dari bukti sekarang | **be going to** (bukan continuous) | *Look! It'**s going to rain**.* |

- **Masa depan vs masa lalu vs sampai sekarang** — lihat **penanda waktunya**:

  | Penanda waktu | Tense | Contoh |
  |---|---|---|
  | *yesterday, last week, two days ago* | **past simple** (Unit 2) | *I **visited** Bogor last week.* |
  | *for two years, since 2019* | **present perfect** (Unit 1) | *I **have lived** here since 2019.* |
  | *tomorrow, next week, in two days* | **going to / present continuous** (Unit 3) | *I'**m visiting** Bogor next week.* |

  Hati-hati: *in* + lamanya waktu = **masa depan** (*in two days* = dua hari lagi), sedangkan
  *two days **ago*** = masa lalu.

## Vocabulary — Holidays, trips & events

**Tujuan:** kata-kata untuk **merencanakan liburan, perjalanan, dan acara**. Pasangkan dengan
Point 1 (*I'm going to book …*) dan Point 2 (*We're flying …*).

Kumpulan kata bertema (Oxford tier A1–A2, dicek di PDF Oxford 3000 by CEFR). Daftar lengkapnya
juga ada di `reference/oxford-vocabulary.md`.

| Word | POS | CEFR | Meaning | Arti | Example |
|------|-----|------|---------|------|---------|
| plan | n, v | A1 | something you decide to do; to decide what to do | rencana; merencanakan | *What are your **plans** for the holiday?* |
| holiday | n | A1 | a time when you do not work or go to school | liburan | *We're going to Bali for the school **holiday**.* |
| ticket | n | A1 | a paper or e-mail that lets you travel or enter | tiket | *I bought the train **tickets** online.* |
| flight | n | A1 | a journey by plane | penerbangan | *Our **flight** is leaving at 6 a.m.* |
| decide | v | A1 | choose what to do after thinking | memutuskan | *We **decided** to go by train.* |
| hope | v | A1 | want something to happen | berharap | *I **hope** the weather is nice tomorrow.* |
| book | v | A2 | arrange to have a seat, room, etc. later | memesan (tiket/kamar) | *I'm going to **book** a hotel tonight.* |
| pack | v | A2 | put things in a bag for a trip | berkemas | *I'm going to **pack** my bag after dinner.* |
| invite | v | A2 | ask someone to come to an event | mengundang | *My cousin **invited** us to her wedding.* |
| celebrate | v | A2 | do something special for an important day | merayakan | *We're **celebrating** Lebaran at my grandmother's house.* |
| wedding | n | A2 | a ceremony when two people marry | pernikahan, resepsi | *The **wedding** is next Saturday.* |
| guest | n | A2 | a person you invite to your home or event | tamu | *About 300 **guests** are coming.* |

> *book* sebagai **kata benda** (buku) = A1; sebagai **kata kerja** (memesan) = A2.

## Reading

**Tujuan:** melihat *going to* dan present continuous untuk masa depan dipakai bersama
**dalam satu teks**. Perhatikan kapan penulis memakai yang mana.

> **Plans for Lebaran**
>
> Hi Maya,
>
> Thank you for your message! Yes, we have big plans for the holiday. My family **is going to
> celebrate** Lebaran in Yogyakarta this year. My father already booked the train tickets, so
> we **are leaving** Jakarta **next Thursday** at 8 a.m. My aunt **is meeting** us at the
> station. We **aren't staying** at a hotel — we**'re staying** at my grandmother's house.
>
> I have a lot of ideas, but I haven't decided everything yet. I**'m going to visit** the
> Prambanan temple, and I**'m going to try** gudeg again. My brother **isn't going to come**
> with us to the temple because he hates hot weather! Also, my cousin Dina **is getting
> married** on the Sunday after Lebaran, so there **are** many guests and a big party.
>
> What **are** you **doing** for the holiday? **Are** you **going to go** back to Medan?
>
> See you soon,
> Rina

*(Sambil membaca, perhatikan: rencana yang **sudah diatur** (tiket, janji di stasiun,
pernikahan) memakai **present continuous** — *are leaving, is meeting, is getting married*;
**niat** yang belum pasti memakai **going to** — *I'm going to visit, I'm going to try*.
Semuanya punya **am/is/are**.)*

## Speaking / Writing phrases

**Tujuan:** kerangka kalimat siap pakai untuk **membicarakan rencana** — tinggal isi bagian *…*.

- **Menyebut niat:** *I'm going to …* · *This year, I'm going to …* · *I'm not going to …*
- **Menyebut janji/jadwal:** *I'm meeting … at … tomorrow.* · *We're leaving on …* · *My …
  is coming next week.*
- **Belum pasti:** *I'm not sure yet, but I'm going to …* · *I hope …* · *Maybe I'm going to …*
- **Alasan:** *I'm going to …, because …* · *We booked the tickets, so we're flying on …*
- **Bertanya:** *What are you doing this weekend?* · *Are you going to …?* · *When are you
  leaving?*

> Selalu tulis **subjek + am/is/are** di setiap klausa (*after that **I'm** going to …*,
> bukan *~~after that going to~~*). Ini kebiasaan yang sama dengan Unit 1 dan 2.

## TOEFL link

**Tujuan:** menunjukkan di bagian tes mana bentuk masa depan benar-benar terpakai.

_Format TOEFL iBT 2026 (lihat `reference/toefl-2026-spec.md`)._

- **Write an Email (Writing):** tugasnya sering minta kamu **mengusulkan rencana** atau
  **menjelaskan apa yang akan kamu lakukan** (*I'm going to …*, *I'm meeting the manager on
  Monday*). Pakai going to/present continuous + penanda waktu yang jelas.
- **Take an Interview (Speaking):** pertanyaan seperti *"What are your plans after
  graduation?"* atau *"What are you going to do this summer?"* dijawab dengan *going to* +
  alasan (*because …*).
- **Listen to a Conversation / Announcement (Listening):** pengumuman kampus sering berisi
  jadwal (*The library **is closing** early on Friday*, *We**'re moving** the meeting to next
  week*). Penanda waktu (*next week, on Friday*) membantu menjawab soal detail.
- **Build a Sentence (Writing):** sering menguji urutan kata pertanyaan masa depan:
  ***What are you going to** do …?* (bukan *~~What you are going to do~~*), dan *be* + V-ing.
